Its own little life

Pip isn't here to entertain you. It just lives here.

It strolls past on its way somewhere, settles in for a nap, or watches something drift by. It never asks anything of you.

Personality

Cat person or dog person?

Pick one and Pip takes on that temperament. Every Pip also gets its own traits, like sleepy, independent and shy, or lively, affectionate and bold.

“Enough.”
  • Independent, affectionate on its own terms

  • Sometimes stalks your cursor, wiggles… and pounces. And misses.

Play bow
  • Easygoing and happy to be petted for longer

Behaviors are based on how real cats and dogs act, drawn from animal-welfare and veterinary sources.

Calm by design

Never in the way.

  • Stays at the bottom edge, off to the side, clear of what you're reading
  • You choose how often it drops by, from about once an hour
  • Quiet mode, snooze for an hour, or turn it off on any site
  • Right-click Pip any time to send it on its way
  • No talking at you. A short hello is opt-in
  • Respects your system's reduced-motion setting
